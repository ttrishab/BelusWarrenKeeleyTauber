##Summary:
#Event- and daily-level polygons for fire event delineation (FIRED) for the US and Canada from 2001-2021. It is preprocessed by the Earth Lab at the University of Colorado Boulder from data collected from the MODIS MCD64A1 burned area product. FIREDpy algorithm converts the raster data into specific fire events. This offers the ability to examine fire spread rate.
